Three ways the other tools are quietly wrong
All of them read the same public JSON feed. None of them read the notes that feed points at.
01 · SCOPE LIVES IN A PDF
487 of 565 remedy rules name no products
They defer to the Chapter 99 U.S. Notes, published only as prose. A tool built on the JSON alone is guessing which goods a tariff covers. We parse the Notes — and where scope is still unresolved we withhold the duty and say so, rather than inventing a figure.
02 · SUSPENDED, STILL PRINTED
Heading 9903.88.16 reads 15% and is not in force
Suspensions live in footnotes, not in the rate column. Applying that heading overstates duty on affected goods by fifteen points. We read the footnotes.
03 · MONEY OWED TO YOU
The IEEPA tariffs were struck down in February 2026
Those provisions are still sitting in the schedule. We report them separately as potentially refundable rather than as duty owed, so you can see what may be recoverable by protest.
